Details — how this page works
Survey Status Definitions
Current: last survey 2022 or later. Partial: 2019-2021. Overdue: 2018 or earlier. Not surveyed: no Keystone REP records found. Properties not surveyed recently will have unreliable Year Due dates.
Consultant Attribution
Surveys attributed to: Savills, Potter Raper, Martin Arnold, FFT, and Internal/Other based on the Surveyor field in Keystone REP.
Component Year Due
Year Due is the scheduled replacement year from Keystone REP. Red = due before 2026, Amber = 2026-2030, Green = after 2030. Multiple records per property use the earliest Year Due.
Coverage Gaps
Not all properties have been surveyed for all components. Counts show only properties with a valid Year Due date recorded in Keystone REP.

Block & Compliance
Communal component priorities · BSR registered high-rise blocks · Royal Borough of Greenwich · 2023–2026
Details — how this page works
Block Priority Score
Each block scores 1 point per communal component category with Year Due ≤2030. Categories: Access & Security, Roof & Structure, Balconies & Rails, Communal Heating, Electrical, Fire Safety, Windows. Maximum 7.
BSR Registration
BSR registration applies to high-rise buildings (18m+ or 7+ storeys). RBG has 70 BSR-registered blocks marked with the building icon. Hover to see the full HRB number.
Communal vs Programme Components
Genuine communal components are always included. Property-level components are included only where ≥80% of properties in the block share an identical Year Due, indicating a coordinated block programme. Labelled Programme.
Statutory Compliance
The six compliance cards (Fire Risk, Legionella, Asbestos, Gas Safety, EICR, Lifts) show Awaiting data until statutory compliance records are provided. These will be populated when data is available.

Retrofit & Sustainability
EPC performance · component renewal priorities · damp risk · programme planning tool · Royal Borough of Greenwich · 2023–2026
Details — how this page works
Priority Score (out of 6)
Each asset group scores 1 point per triggered indicator: Windows due ≤2030 (≥20% of properties), Front Door (≥20%), Loft Insulation (≥20%), CWI (≥5%), EWI (≥5%), and EPC D or below (≥30%). Higher score = stronger retrofit case.
EPC & SAP Data
EPC ratings (A-G) and SAP scores come from the stock master file. SAP is the Standard Assessment Procedure energy efficiency score. Properties rated F or G are fuel poverty risk and fail Decent Homes Criterion 4.
Component Renewal Dates
Year Due dates come from Keystone REP stock condition surveys. The % columns show the proportion of properties where that component is due for replacement by 2030. Only surveyed properties are included.

Decent Homes Standard
Property-level assessment against the 4 Decent Homes criteria · LAHS reporting · Royal Borough of Greenwich
Details — how this page works
Criterion 1 — HHSRS Hazards
FAIL if: Damp RAG = Red OR EPC F/G (excess cold risk). All tenures assessed.
Criterion 2 — State of Repair
FAIL if: any key component Year Due is before 2025. Leasehold excluded as council is not responsible for internal component repairs in leasehold properties.
Criterion 3 — Modern Facilities
FAIL if: kitchen over 20 years old OR bathroom over 30 years old. Leasehold excluded as council is not responsible for kitchen or bathroom replacements.
Criterion 4 — Thermal Comfort
FAIL if: SAP below 35 OR EPC F/G OR heating overdue. All tenures assessed. A property fails Decent Homes if it fails ANY ONE of the four criteria.

Assessment Methodology
Criterion 1 — HHSRS (Category 1 Hazards)
FAIL if: Damp RAG = Red (active damp/mould hazard) OR EPC rating F or G (excess cold risk)
Criterion 2 — Reasonable State of Repair
FAIL if: Any key component is overdue (Year Due before 2025). Components: Windows, Doors, Roof, Boiler, Wiring, External Wall. Leasehold properties excluded — council is not responsible for internal components in leasehold.
Criterion 3 — Modern Facilities
FAIL if: Kitchen over 20 years old OR Bathroom over 30 years old (based on Keystone Year Due and standard lifespans). Leasehold properties excluded — council is not responsible for kitchen/bathroom replacements in leasehold.
Criterion 4 — Thermal Comfort
FAIL if: SAP score below 35 OR EPC rating F or G OR primary heating system overdue
A property is classed as Non-Decent if it fails ANY one of the four criteria above. Data source: Keystone REP survey, ATT attribute survey, reactive repairs data, stock master EPC/SAP scores.
Programme Builder
Capital investment programme · 2026/27 to 2030/31 · Royal Borough of Greenwich
Details — how this page works
2-Year Bundling Logic
If a component is due within 2 years of the earliest due component for the same asset group, it is brought forward into the same programme year to reduce mobilisation costs. Costs are fixed at the original due year (no inflation penalty).
Cost Calculation
Cost = properties where the component is due x unit cost from HbH_Component_Costs.xlsx. Only properties with that component due are included, not all properties in the asset group.
Budget Status Badges
In budget: cumulative cost fits within budget. Partial: this asset group straddles the budget limit. Over: cumulative cost exceeds budget and the group would be deferred. Edit budget inputs to model different scenarios live.
Unit Costs & Budgets
Default costs and budgets load from HbH_Component_Costs.xlsx in Data/Costs/. Edit the blue cells and rerun build_portal.py to update with real figures. Budget inputs on this page update the programme instantly without rerunning.
